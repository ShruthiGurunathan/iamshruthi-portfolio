it’s nice to meet you

My name is Shruthi.

I’m a Sydney-based UX/UI designer with a background that moves between architecture, jewellery and digital design. Living across Brisbane, Melbourne and Sydney has made me curious about the ways people navigate places, products and everyday experiences.

I’ve always been drawn to the details: how something feels in your hand, how a space guides you, why an interface makes sense without explanation. That curiosity eventually brought me to interaction design.

I like making digital experiences that feel intuitive, meaningful and human with enough personality that they don’t disappear into the background.

Outside design, I’m usually wandering through a market or gallery, styling something, making something, listening to jazz or finding a good oat-milk coffee and croissant.
