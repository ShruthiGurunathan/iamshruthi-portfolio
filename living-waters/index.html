03 Living Waters

A public installation concept that invites visitors to pause and play together at Sydney Fish Market.

about

At Sydney Fish Market, people move quickly between the market and the waterfront. We explored how a public installation could give them a reason to pause, with enough room to watch, join in or keep walking. I helped develop the experience and used prototype feedback to refine how visitors would find, use and understand the interaction pads.

Public installation concept · Blackwattle Bay, Sydney · 2026
