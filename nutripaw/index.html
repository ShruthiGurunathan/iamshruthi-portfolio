02 NutriPaw

Helping busy pet owners keep track of daily food, treats and activity.

about

NutriPaw is a new mobile app concept for helping busy owners track their pets’ daily care. My contribution covered research, user flows, wireframes and UI prototyping, informed by five research respondents.

01 discovery

Research with five respondents highlighted busy routines and difficulty tracking treats and activity. These findings shaped an app concept that brings daily care into one place.

Stats, food and exercise · App navigation

defining the daily plan

Owners described difficulty keeping track of treats. This informed a daily stats view with a treat counter, alongside water and exercise tracking, while the daily plan brings care tasks together.

User flow · From onboarding to daily care

02 prototyping

Low-fi exploration · Pet setup and daily plan

High-fi concept · Pet setup and daily plan

Low-fi wireframes explored pet setup and the daily task list. High-fi prototyping developed those ideas through progress cues, photography and consistent navigation.

A teal and lime identity gives the new app a recognisable visual language. Reusable navigation, fields, counters and progress indicators support consistency across the experience. Next steps would include usability testing the treat-logging flow with pet owners to see how quickly they can log a treat, then tracking whether they return to log treats on subsequent days.
