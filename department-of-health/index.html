01 Department of Health

Making health information easier to find through clearer topic groups and more consistent navigation.

about

A concept redesign focused on making health information easier to find. Research with 10+ users informed the navigation, topic groups and responsive screens. I contributed across all stages with the team, from research, testing and information architecture to wireframes, UI design and prototyping.

We mapped how people find a health topic and reach relevant information, using clearer groups to guide the journey.

Original Resources page · Heuristic audit excerpt

before the redesign

The original Resources page had competing search bars and inconsistent selection cues. Important filters were also difficult to find.

These findings shaped clearer topic groups and more consistent navigation cues.
